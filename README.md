# Which Committee Are You?

A 16Personalities-style quiz for You and A Pusa members: 20 statements that match you to the Feeding, Marketing, Logistics or Health committee.

**Open:** https://s3ryz000.github.io/Committee/

1. Open the link on the projector and click **Start hosting**.
2. Members scan the QR code, enter their name and UA&P email, and take the quiz on their phones.
3. Results appear live on the big screen, grouped by committee.
4. Click **Download results (CSV)** before ending the session.

No code? Anyone can click **Take it on your own** to get a result without joining a session.
